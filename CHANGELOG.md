## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Ha Ha Ha, a tempo-synced stutter and beat repeat, as a VST3 and CLAP plugin for macOS and Windows.
- A treadle that grabs the last slice on your host's grid and repeats it in time, held down or latched, with Rate from 1 bar to 1/32 (triplets included) and a Mix knob.
- Fade, Bend and Roll, to let the repeats die away, slow into a tape stop or speed up into a riser, and get shorter as they go.
- Eight presets, from Stutter to Big loop, stepped through in a window on the pedal.
- The version is printed on the pedal, so you can tell which build your host loaded.
